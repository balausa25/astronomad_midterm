About Our Solar System

Our solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits around it, including 8 planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

The 8 Planets

The planets are divided into three groups:

  • Terrestrial planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars
  • Gas giants: Jupiter, Saturn
  • Ice giants: Uranus, Neptune

How to Observe Planets

  1. Choose a clear night with minimal light pollution.
  2. Use a telescope or binoculars for better view.
  3. Download a star map app to locate planets.
  4. Look for bright steady lights — those are planets!

Quick Facts

Earth is the only planet known to support life. Mars has the tallest volcano in the solar system (Olympus Mons, 21 km tall). Saturn's rings are made of ice and rock particles.
